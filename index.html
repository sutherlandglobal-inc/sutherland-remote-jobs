FAQ

Common questions

Do I need previous professional experience?
No. We have roles suitable for beginners. For creative roles like logo design or translation, we simply ask that you have a good grasp of the fundamentals. We provide clear guidelines for every project.
How many hours a week do I need to work?
This is a flexible opportunity. You can work part-time or full-time depending on your availability and the project requirements. You are in control of your schedule.
Are these independent contractor roles?
Yes. You will be working on a freelance/project basis. You will be responsible for your own taxes based on your local laws.
How do I apply?
Tap any “Apply” or “Get Job Details” button on this page. It will open a direct WhatsApp chat with our hiring team. Send a brief message indicating which role you are interested in.
