FAQ

Questions before you apply

Do I need previous experience?
Not necessarily. Sutherland provides paid training and structured onboarding for every remote role. What matters most is your communication style, reliability, and willingness to learn.
Is equipment provided?
Yes. For most remote roles, Sutherland supplies the necessary equipment. You'll need a reliable internet connection — typically at least 10 Mbps download and 2 Mbps upload.
Are these full-time roles?
The roles listed here are full-time remote positions. Shift patterns vary by team and client, and your recruiter will walk you through the options during screening.
Where is Sutherland headquartered?
Sutherland is headquartered in Rochester, New York, and operates delivery centers across 20+ countries. This role is fully remote, so you can work from anywhere within the hiring country.
How do I apply?
Tap any “Apply” button on this page to open WhatsApp, add us as a contact and send a short message with the role you're interested in. Our team will reply with the next steps.
